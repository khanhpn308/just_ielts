# FALSE vs NOT GIVEN

## Core distinction
- **FALSE:** the information in the passage contradicts the statement.
- **NOT GIVEN:** the passage does not supply enough information to decide whether the statement is true or false.
- **TRUE:** the passage supports the statement.

## When to apply
The learner labels an unsupported claim FALSE or mistakes contrary evidence for a missing statement.

## Correction procedure
1. Decompose the statement into subject, claim, qualifiers and scope.
2. Locate the related passage text; do not use outside knowledge.
3. Test for positive support, direct contradiction, or insufficient information.
4. Show exactly which words establish the choice, or why information is insufficient.

## Common traps
Quantifiers (`all`, `some`), negation, time frame, comparisons and modality (`may`, `must`).

## Boundary
Do not infer a contradiction solely from the absence of supporting evidence. For **YES/NO/NOT GIVEN**, compare the *writer's views or claims*, not merely factual statements.
