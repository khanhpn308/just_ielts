# Evidence-Based Answering

## Principle
An answer must be justified by a specific part of the passage, not by what seems likely from background knowledge.

## When to apply
- A learner selects an answer because it sounds reasonable.
- A learner cannot identify where the passage supports their choice.

## Correction procedure
1. Restate the question's exact claim.
2. Find the relevant sentence(s) in the passage.
3. Compare meaning, scope, negation and qualifiers with the proposed answer.
4. If the evidence is absent or ambiguous, report the uncertainty; do not fabricate support.

## Boundaries
The passage and a checked answer key outrank this note. This principle does not imply that every question has a single directly quoted sentence; some questions require combining supported information.

## Transfer exercise
Ask the learner to highlight the smallest evidence span for an unfamiliar question and explain the logical link.
